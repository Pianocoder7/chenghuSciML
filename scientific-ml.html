Generative Reconstruction

Generative reconstruction of coupled flow and deformation from sparse measurements.

I used conditional flow matching to reconstruct coupled flow and structural deformation. Comparing multiple reconstructions helped identify where uncertainty increased as observations became sparser.

Reconstruction from sparse observations

The benchmark couples flow past a cylinder and deformation of a flexible beam. We used conditional flow matching to estimate the full state from a limited set of measurements.

Uncertainty across space and time

Multiple reconstructions reveal where sparse observations leave the flow least constrained. I evaluated uncertainty across the domain and tracked its variation over time.
