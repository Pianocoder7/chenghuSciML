Grand Prize · National College Student Medical Innovation Competition

Experimental and computational modeling of pediatric upper airway mechanics.

I developed an experimental platform and used patient-specific simulations to study pediatric airway collapse and how mandibular advancement affects airway deformation.

Airway anatomy and experiment

We built a physical airway model to establish flow conditions for a patient-specific simulation. The images show how the anatomical model and experiment relate.

Effect of mandibular advancement

Paired simulations show how advancing the mandible changes the airflow and airway wall deformation. The comparison uses the same set of cross sections.
