Early Bird Flight

Aerodynamic modeling of gliding in the Early Cretaceous bird Confuciusornis.

From fossil to flight model

Our team reconstructed Confuciusornis from fossil drawings. I used aerodynamic coefficients from CFD to simulate the bird’s descent.

Gliding and landing

I compared descent trajectories for different glide strategies to assess whether this early bird could land without sustained flapping.
